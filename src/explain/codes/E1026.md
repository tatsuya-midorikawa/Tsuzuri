# Compile-time constant error

## What it covers
A constant or `const def` cannot be evaluated at compile time: evaluation traps (division by zero, signed overflow), constants form a cycle, evaluation exceeds a step or depth limit, a `const def` signature is not concrete or not made of value types, its body uses something that const evaluation does not support, or the constants' static data exceeds 4 MiB.

## Fixes
- Remove the trap: use a nonzero divisor and values that do not overflow.
- Break a cycle between constants, or make one of them a function.
- From a `const def`, call only `const def` functions, fully applied, without pipes, borrows, or `@checked` operations.
- Give a `const def` a concrete signature of value types, without type variables or constraints.
- Move work that const evaluation does not support into a runtime `def` or `let`.
- Shrink large constants, or read one through a `let` binding of its value.
