# Borrow that outlives its owner or crosses a tzroutine

## What it covers
A borrow could be used after its owner is gone: returned from a block or function, stored in a field or a longer-lived variable, carried into a tzroutine or through a channel, or kept in a `Mutex.with_lock` or `Parallel` result. It also covers values a tzroutine cannot take (an `Rc`, values that are not `Send` or `Sync`) and named regions that are undeclared, unused, or mismatched. The kind `borrow_across_async_boundary` (a borrow kept across a step of an `Async` computation) is no longer reported: TZ13 removed `Async`.

## Fixes
- Return or store an owned value: copy with `clone_string`, `Array.copy`, or `List.copy`.
- Declare the owner in an outer block so that it lives as long as the borrow; bind a temporary with `let` before borrowing it.
- Give tzroutines, channels, and parallel callbacks owned values; share data across tzroutines with `Arc` instead of `Rc`.
- Declare a named region after the declaration's name, use it in a borrowed type such as `ref {r} T`, and remove regions you do not use.
