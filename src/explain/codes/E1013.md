# Borrow that outlives its owner or crosses a task

## What it covers
A borrow could be used after its owner is gone: returned from a block or function, stored in a field or a longer-lived variable, carried into a task, across an async step, or through a channel, or kept in a `Mutex.with_lock` or `Parallel` result. It also covers values a task cannot take (an `Rc`, values that are not `Send` or `Sync`) and named regions that are undeclared, unused, or mismatched.

## Fixes
- Return or store an owned value: copy with `clone_string`, `Array.copy`, or `List.copy`.
- Declare the owner in an outer block so that it lives as long as the borrow; bind a temporary with `let` before borrowing it.
- Give tasks, channels, and parallel callbacks owned values; share data across tasks with `Arc` instead of `Rc`.
- Declare a named region after the declaration's name, use it in a borrowed type such as `ref {r} T`, and remove regions you do not use.
