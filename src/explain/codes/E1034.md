# Contract that could not be proved

## What it covers
`tsuzuri prove` or `--contracts prove` could not prove a function's contract lines with the SMT solver: the solver found a counterexample (arguments for which the body traps or an `@ensures` line is false where the `@requires` lines hold), the function uses something outside the supported subset (loops, strings, arrays, closures, recursion, floating point), the solver did not decide in time, failed, or was not found.

## Fixes
- Fix the body or the contract that the counterexample breaks; run the reported call to see it trap.
- Strengthen the `@requires` lines so that the inputs that break the body are excluded.
- Keep proved functions to integers, `bool`, tuples, records and unions without payloads, and calls of such functions.
- Raise `--prove-timeout`, or simplify the contract.
- Install z3 or cvc5, or set `TSUZURI_SMT` to the solver.
